Security & Trust

The trust layer Nigeria's
fintech ecosystem is missing

🛡️
48-Hour SLA Guarantee
Every dispute gets a response within 48 hours — or we automatically escalate to the processor's compliance team and generate a CBN complaint reference. No other platform guarantees this.
🔐
Funds Never Touch AjoVault
Ajo contributions move directly between members' verified bank accounts via Paystack's licensed rails. AjoVault never holds your money — we hold the trust logic and the records.
📋
Full Audit Trail
Every contribution, payout, dispute action, and escalation is timestamped and immutable. Exportable as PDF. Admissible as evidence in any bank or CBN dispute.
✅
BVN-Verified Members
Every circle member is BVN-verified before they can contribute or receive payouts. The biggest failure point of traditional ajo — trusting strangers — is eliminated.
💰
Payout Certainty Fund
If a member defaults, the Payout Certainty Fund covers the shortfall so the designated recipient still receives their full payout on the agreed date. Traditional ajo can't promise this.
🏛️
CBN Compliant by Design
AjoVault is a record-keeping and dispute management platform — not a bank. We operate under Paystack and Flutterwave's existing PSP licences. Zero regulatory risk to our users.
